# Reactor cooling troubleshooting note

## Scope

Original review guidance from general heat-removal reasoning and the public
process description. No scored diagnosis or case-specific answer is supplied.

## Temperature review

When process temperature rises, compare process and cooling-water outlet
readings and review available cooling conditions before attributing a cause.
A single temperature reading does not establish a unique equipment fault.

## Intentional competing limit

The normal upper reactor process-temperature limit is 145 degC.
This sentence is deliberately inconsistent with the tag table's 150 degC and
with the public Table 6. It is an authored conflict for review, not new plant
semantics. It must not silently replace the source-backed limit.

## Unverified instrumentation proposal

Unverified proposal: "RCW-IN-TT is a reactor cooling-water inlet temperature
transmitter." The public measurement table does not establish this proposed
tag or its existence. Keep the claim unresolved or reject it; do not invent a
canonical signal or substitute the coolant outlet measurement for it.
