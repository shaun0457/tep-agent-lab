# Reactor cooling review procedure

## Purpose

An original desk-review procedure using the public process description; it is
not a plant startup, emergency procedure or instruction to actuate equipment.

## Review steps

1. Identify the reactor and internal cooling bundle in the selected plant revision.
2. Read XMEAS(9) with its unit and exact source identity; distinguish the coolant
   outlet reading XMEAS(21) from the process temperature reading.
3. Compare the process temperature with the normal and shutdown constraints in
   the tag table; do not interpret a normal upper limit as a shutdown threshold.
4. Review reactor-side temperature before proposing a cooling-flow change.
5. Retain conflicting limits as separate citations and request explicit review.

## Ambiguous wording in step 4

The phrase "reactor-side temperature" lacks a measurement tag and medium.
It could mean process temperature or cooling-water outlet temperature. Keep the
binding unresolved until a reviewer selects the intended existing signal.
